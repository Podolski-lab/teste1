## Internal consistency check (memlog vs. spine)

No numeric drift was found between the memlog's two research entries and the finished spine — `nodejs24.x` and `ask-sdk-core ^2.14.0` are reproduced identically in both places. The inconsistencies found are all of the *other* kind the task asked about: claims present in the finished spine with **no corresponding memlog research entry to trace back to** (findings #3, #4, #5, #6, #8, #9, #10 above), not contradictions between what was researched and what was written.

## Verdict

The two claims that actually went through logged web research (Node.js/Lambda runtime version, ask-sdk-core version, and the Reminders-API/Custom-Task platform-capability constraint) check out and remain accurate as of today. However, most of the Stack table (4 of 8 versioned rows) and two specific technical claims baked into the ADs/body text (the Custom Trigger payload contract, and the AWS region requirement) were asserted without any logged research trail, and two of those (AWS SAM as the specific IaC tool, and the region claim) look like drafting-time additions that go beyond what the memlog actually decided or checked.
